This privacy policy applies to the Seb7a mobile application (hereinafter the “Application”) published on the Google Play Store. It describes what data is collected and how it is used — including for advertising purposes.

The Application is developed and managed by the developer of Seb7a. For any questions related to this policy, you can contact us using the address provided at the end of this document.

2 Data Collected

🔒 No user account required. The Application only uses anonymous authentication provided by Firebase. No name, email address, or phone number is collected.

Storage in Our Database (Firestore)

Your anonymous identifier (UID) is used as the document key in Firestore. This document contains your FCM token and certain usage statistics linked to that identifier. This data is used to:

  • Send you personalized notifications or targeted advertising messages
  • Analyze usage behavior for marketing purposes
  • Improve campaign targeting
⚠️ Advertising use: Usage data (in-app behavior, frequency, preferences) and your FCM token may be used to send you advertising or promotional notifications.

Data NOT Collected

  • First name, last name, email address, or phone number
  • Precise GPS location
  • Contacts, photos, or files on your device
  • Payment data

4 Purposes of Processing

  • Ensure proper functioning and stability of the Application

  • Send reminders and notifications related to app usage (dhikr, prayers)

Advertising and Marketing Purposes

  • Send advertising or promotional push notifications via FCM, using the token stored in Firestore

6 User Rights

The Application only uses anonymous identifiers (Firebase UID, FCM token) and does not collect names, emails, or phone numbers. In the absence of personal identity data, it is not possible to exercise individual access or rectification rights. The only way to stop data collection is to uninstall the Application.

Uninstalling results in:

  • Invalidation of your FCM token (notifications will no longer be sent)
  • Deletion of the Application's local data on your device

Note: Data already collected and stored on Firebase servers (token, statistics, anonymous identifier) is not deleted when uninstalling and remains retained indefinitely.

7 Data Sharing and Transfer with Third Parties

Your data is transmitted to Firebase services (Google LLC) for the purposes described above.

Aggregated and anonymized statistics from Analytics and Firestore (usage behavior, audience segments, usage frequency, device type, etc.) may be shared, transferred, or sold to third-party partners for commercial, advertising, or analytical purposes.

This data is strictly anonymized and aggregated: it cannot be used to identify an individual user. No personally identifiable data (name, email, phone number, precise location) is included in this sharing, in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

8 Permissions Requested

  • INTERNET — required for Firebase synchronization.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+) — for sending functional and advertising notifications. You may refuse or revoke this permission at any time from your device settings.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — to restart scheduled reminders after device reboot (if applicable).

9 Data Retention Period

Collected data (FCM token, usage statistics, anonymous identifier) is retained indefinitely on Firebase servers. Uninstalling the Application does not delete this data from servers.

Firebase may apply its own retention periods in accordance with its privacy policy.
